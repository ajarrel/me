§ D

Market Position

The Harrisburg / NE Concord submarket has tightened into a wide dispersion pattern: EXR Veterans sits well above the rest of the comp set across every CC and Conv line (often 40–80% above median), while PSA Stough, EXR Univ City and Mr. Storage compete aggressively on the low end. SafeNest plays the premium-indie middle. Public Storage Stough is running very loud promotional pricing on small CC ($16–$23 for a 5×5 CC) — likely soft occupancy or an algorithmic push given the multi-level format.

Subject (NC-49 S) is broadly under-priced on smaller units and 10×15+, but appropriately positioned on 10×20 CC and parking. The 5×5 CC line in particular ($18 street) is an outlier — it appears the subject has anchored to the $8 promo floor for a sustained period, and the gap to a normalized $50–55 market level is the single largest revenue lever in the matrix. Cabarrus County remains the highest-risk market in ASSI's portfolio given Charlotte-area supply growth, but the dispersion in this comp set suggests there is room to move on the smaller units without becoming the market high.
